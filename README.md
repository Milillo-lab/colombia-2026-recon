# Colombia 2026: remote sensing reconnaissance

**Site: https://milillo-lab.github.io/colombia-2026-recon/**

A dated record of the M7.4 earthquake of 10 August 2026 in western Colombia (USGS us6000tjl2): what agencies,
charters and companies published, and what the Milillo Lab at the University of Houston acquired and did in
remote support of the EEFIT Colombia 2026 field mission.

The page is built by AGENT_RECON, the lab's reconnaissance agent, from a publication ledger. The agent gathers,
processes and proposes; every item on this page was approved and released by a named person, and the page shows
who. Results that belong to papers in preparation are held until those papers are submitted.

| file | what |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | the site, self-contained |
| `feed.json` | the same items as data: date, source, summary, link, places, provenance |

This repository is written by `recon publish deploy`; edits made here by hand are overwritten on the next
deployment, except this README.
